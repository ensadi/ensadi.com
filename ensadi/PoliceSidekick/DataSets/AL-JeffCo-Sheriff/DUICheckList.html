• Of course document your reason for stopping, the stop and any sobriety indicators.
  • Write Two citations, one for DUI per code book and one for reckless driving or some other offense that caused you to stop them. Reckless driving is the best reason. Put Jailed in the sign line on the citation.
  • Call for 10-51 and do pull in report including inventory of vehicle.
  • Do an arrest report for DUI and document very well in your narrative what happened! This will make or break your case.
  • Put both your citations and numbers on the arrest report in the appropriate section.
  • Transport them to the jail calling ahead to get the Dragar warmed up.
  • Have a jail deputy perform the Dragar and get the paperwork to send to the state with his licenses. Take his drivers license. Get your Dragar report and include in the narrative what he blew.
  • Take the citations, jail paperwork, arrest report, drivers license to the magistrate. You do not need a deposition because your citation is your warrant.
  • Keep copies of everything because you will need it for court later on.
