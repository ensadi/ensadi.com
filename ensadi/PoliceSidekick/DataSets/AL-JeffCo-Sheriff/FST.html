Always demonstrate all tests to the subject prior to starting them


One Leg Stand

Ask the subject to stand still with arms down by his or her sides. Then ask the subject to raise one foot about six inches off the ground and count until told to stop. The counting should go for about 30 seconds. Be sure the arms are at the sides at all times. Look for the test being stopped early, the foot is put down, the counting done wrong or not at all, or the arms used for balance.

Walk and Turn

This is a test of the subject's physical performance and ability to follow instructions. Ask the subject to walk a line taking nine heel to toe steps. At the end of the line or number of steps, the subject must turn by making small steps with one foot only and walk back in the same manner to the beginning of the line. Look for balance problems, taking the wrong number of steps, turning incorrectly at the end of the line, or stopping prematurely.

Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus

Ask the subject to hold his or her head very still and follow an object (pen or light) with his or her eyes only. Be sure to stress that the subject may not turn the head to follow the object but only the eyes. Look for an involuntary jerking of the eyes that should appear at the limits of the eyes. The jerking should be most apparent as the subject looks right and left.

Recite Alphabet

Ask the subject to recite a portion of the alphabet. Make sure he or she does not sing the alphabet song. Look for problems reciting.
